# Luminous alchemist UI

`../scripts/board_skin.gd` is original code-native material artwork for this prototype, marked CC0; the complete CC0 notice in `relics/LICENSE.txt` also accompanies it. Its glass gradients, clipped corners, gold edges and native control glyphs use no external raster art. `../scripts/board_quality_fx.gd` and the selection stitches in main.gd are original procedural presentation code, with no new downloaded effect asset or audio.

The approved `alchemist-portrait-v1.png`, `training-jelly-golem-v1.png`, pigment atlas and three relic SVGs remain unmodified. Normal play keeps the portrait at the bottom. The successful-skill strip crops only the supplied portrait's face region (250,240,590,280); it does not create full-body artwork. Selection strokes use actual frozen contacts. Damage and healing numerals use the canonical committed plan.

Fonts are unmodified official Black Han Sans and Pretendard originals. See `fonts/README.md`, `fonts/SOURCES.json`, the two complete OFL license files and `fonts/SHA256SUMS`. Black Han is used for display words and large counts; Pretendard is body/emphasis and the runtime fallback for missing display symbols. No DNF font, paid art, copied commercial-game art or new raster generation was used.
